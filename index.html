Netplay+

Achievements, medals and an in-game overlay for Slippi netplay.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or later · requires Slippi Dolphin

What it does

Achievements & Clout

Hundreds of achievements for stages, characters, combos, and milestones. Each one rewards Clout, and a leaderboard to see how you stack up.

Ingame Overlay

Live in-game quick chat with other Netplay+ users, medal/achievement notifications, playercards, and more. All without a second window or alt-tabbing.

Combo Detection

Recognises signature kills as they happen. Every stomp knee, Ken combo, rest, all tracked so you can get rewarded for hitting your sick combo.

Show Your Style

Avatars, titles, flairs and colours, all earned. Show off what you have actually done.

Challenges

Weekly challenges, a daily bingo board, and persistent challenges like Iron Man for when you want a real goal.

Friends & blocking

See who's online, and keep high ping or toxic players out of your unranked matchmaking.

Getting started

  1. Download the installer and run it. It installs for the current user, so no admin prompt.
  2. Open Netplay+, your connect code should be automatically detected.
  3. Play on Slippi as normal. Netplay+ reads your live game and replays, and tracks everything by itself.
Updates are automatic. Netplay+ checks on launch and installs new versions in the background.
